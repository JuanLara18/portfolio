02Foundations

Frontier AI. Engineered for production.

Engineer first

The brand speaks as an engineer who takes state-of-the-art research to production at enterprise scale. Engineering leads; research is the proof of depth.

Facts over adjectives

Concrete systems, numbers and outcomes. No hype words, no self-praise. The visual system follows the same restraint: few colours, one accent per role.

One system, every surface

The site, share cards, LinkedIn, GitHub, documents and slides come from one source: brand/tokens.json. Nothing is redrawn by hand.

Positioning, voice, bios and the forbidden-phrase list: docs/brand.md §1–9. This book covers the visual identity (§10).

03Signature

A J drawn as a graph, closed by a full stop.

The symbol

One edge and two ink nodes form the J. The amber node sits where a full stop would: the sentence is finished, the system is shipped.

The full stop

It is the brand's recurring device. It closes the tagline, every page and post title, and is the single amber node in any graph.

The name

IBM Plex Serif Medium, upright, tracking −1.2 %, outlined in the files. Never italic: italic belongs to the voice, the name is a fact.

04Construction

Regular cut
Stroke 4.2 · ink nodes r 4.6 · amber node r 5.4. Lockups and anything above 32 px.

09Colour roles

Each colour has one job

primary 188°

Links, active state, labels, the first line of the hero, Field Notes.

harmonic 225°

Graph edges, chips, Research Notes, names in code, duotone mid-tones.

contrast 38°

The logo node and the period that closes a title. Nothing else.

muted

Secondary text, metadata, the Curiosities category.

Why three hues

Cyan and cobalt are neighbours (188° and 225°), so they harmonise; amber sits opposite (38°) and pulls the eye to one thing at a time.

Categories

Field Notes in primary, Research Notes in harmonic, Curiosities in muted. Colour follows the positioning weight.

Never

Per-company, per-pillar or decorative colours. Red, amber-500 and green exist only as UI states. No hex values in components.

10Typography

IBM Plex: one superfamily, three roles

AaIBM Plex Serif

Voice and name. Titles in italic 600, sections upright 600, the name upright 500.

AaIBM Plex Sans

Reading and interface. 400 for text at 18 px / 1.7, 500 for controls.

AaIBM Plex Mono

Code, labels and metadata. Labels in uppercase with +12 % tracking.

Serif, Sans and Mono share one skeleton: measured x-heights 0.520 / 0.520 / 0.515 of the em, so they sit on one line without adjustment. Plex has no optical-size axis; large titles are tuned with weight and negative tracking. Licence: SIL Open Font License.
